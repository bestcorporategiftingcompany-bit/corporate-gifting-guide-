## How to Select the Right Corporate Gift

Before placing a corporate gifting order, businesses should consider a few important questions:

### Who is receiving the gift?

An employee welcome kit may require different products compared with a VIP client gift.

### What is the occasion?

The product selection should match the occasion, whether it is onboarding, recognition, a corporate event, a festival, or client appreciation.

### What is the budget?

Set a clear per-unit budget before selecting products. This makes it easier to shortlist suitable options.

### Is branding required?

If the gift is intended to promote the company, consider logo printing, engraving, customised packaging, or other branding options.

### What quantity is required?

For bulk corporate gifting, discuss quantity, customisation, production time, packaging, and delivery requirements before confirming the order.
